## IOS

### 1. Promotional Text
Send USDC in seconds with a simple wallet flow, live balance view, and swipe-to-send confirmation.

### 2. Description
AFIKA is a simple mobile wallet built to help people send and receive digital dollars with less friction.

With AFIKA, users can:

- Sign in quickly with Apple
- View their balance from a clean home screen
- Send USDC with a guided step-by-step flow
- Enter a recipient wallet address and confirm with a swipe
- Track recent transactions in one place
- Receive funds using their wallet details

AFIKA is designed to make digital money feel familiar, fast, and easy to use from your phone.

### 3. What's New in This Version
- Improved onboarding with a cleaner Sign in with Apple entry flow
- Refined home screen with clearer balance and transaction visibility
- Updated send flow with recipient entry, amount input, and swipe-to-send confirmation
- Added smoother in-progress and success states after sending USDC
- General performance and stability improvements

### 4. Keywords
wallet,usdc,stablecoin,send money,receive money,digital dollars,payments,crypto wallet,mobile wallet,remittance

### 5. App Review Notes
Test flow includes Sign in with Apple, wallet balance, send, receive, transaction history, and send confirmation. The current app experience shown in the build focuses on USDC transfers using a recipient wallet address, amount entry, and a swipe-to-send action.

## ANDROID

### 1. Short Description
Send and receive USDC with a simple mobile wallet built for speed.

### 2. Full Description
AFIKA is a mobile wallet for simple USDC payments.

The app is designed around a clean, guided flow that helps users move digital dollars with confidence.

With AFIKA, users can:

- Sign in quickly
- Check their balance on the home screen
- Send USDC through a simple step-by-step transfer flow
- Enter a wallet address for the recipient

- Receive funds using their wallet details

What makes AFIKA useful:

- Clean mobile-first interface
- Fast send flow

- Built for practical everyday digital payments

## SHORT LAUNCH SCRIPT

AFIKA is a simple mobile wallet for sending and receiving USDC.

From the first screen, the experience is built to feel clear and direct. Users can sign in, check their balance, send funds, receive funds, and review transactions in a clean mobile flow.

The send experience is especially simple: enter a recipient wallet address, type the amount, swipe to confirm, and track the transfer through completion.

AFIKA is built for people who want digital dollar payments to feel practical, familiar, and easy to use on mobile.
